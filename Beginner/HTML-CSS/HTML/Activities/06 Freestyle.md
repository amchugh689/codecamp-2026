## Final Project: Build Your Own Webpage!

Through the lessons above, you should have gained enough information to better understand what makes a website work - and more importantly, how it is structured.

Now, the floor is yours.  

In the sandbox provided, use the information gained to create your own basic webpage.

### Ensure to include:
- Headers  
- Paragraphs  
- Images  
- Links  
- Divs  

---

### Stuck for some ideas?

Here are some examples:

- **Personal Portfolio Page**  
  - Create a web page to showcase your interests, hobbies, and achievements.  
  - Include sections like "About Me," "Projects," "Hobbies," and "Contact Information."

- **Favorite Book/Movie Review**  
  - Write reviews of your favorite books or movies.  
  - Include sections for a summary, characters, personal opinions, and ratings.

- **Local Tourism Guide**  
  - Promote your city or a local tourist attraction.  
  - Include sections for history, key attractions, maps, and visitor tips.

- **Recipe Page**  
  - Share your favorite recipes.  
  - Include ingredients, step-by-step instructions, and images of the finished dish.

- **Music Band Fan Page**  
  - Create a fan page for your favorite music band or artist.  
  - Include biography, discography, upcoming concerts, and a fan gallery.

- **Environmental Awareness Page**  
  - Raise awareness about environmental issues.  
  - Include sections on climate change, recycling tips, and how to reduce your carbon footprint.

- **Sports Team Page**  
  - Create a page for your favorite sports team.  
  - Include team history, player profiles, match schedules, and the latest news.

- **Tech Gadgets Review**  
  - Review the latest tech gadgets or apps.  
  - Include features, pros and cons, pricing, and comparisons with other products.

- **School Club/Organisation Page**  
  - Develop a page for a school club or organisation you're part of.  
  - Include information about the club, upcoming events, and membership details.
