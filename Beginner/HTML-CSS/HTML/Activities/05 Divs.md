Try It Now!!
Let's now add a `<div>` to your webpage!

Within the `<body>` element of your html document, add an opening and closing `<div>` tag around your `<h>` and `<p>` elements. Give this div a class name of, 'myDiv'.

As divs cannot be viewed until styling has been applied, paste the following into your `<head>` element. This will allow for you to view the boundaries of your div. 

The following stylings will add a light-blue background to your div, with a red border and will center all of the text within it.

```html
<style>
  .myDiv{
  border: 5px outset red;
  background-color: lightblue;
  text-align: center;
  }
</style>
```


We will be focusing more on CSS in later tutorials so feel free to come back and play around with the stylings once we have studied it!

Refresh your browser and view the new changes to your page!

Useful Tip!
To get a better understanding of the effects a `<div>` can have on your webpage, add some additional text tags outside of your div and note the difference in styling and format between those inside, and outside the div.
